# cp-core-spec

This repository contains the immutable core principles of the Contribution Protocol.


The Contribution Protocol is released as open source,
allowing independent implementations of CP in different regions, forks,
as well as experiments and derivative works based on its core principles.


License: CC BY 4.0


## Kan – The Closed Cycle

CP was born from sorrow.
Prayer is embedded into the structure,
forming a closed cycle called Kan.

So that no one will have to carry the same sorrow again.


## Definition of Kan

Kan refers to a state in which,
within a completed cyclical structure,
hidden strategy becomes embedded within the structure itself,

allowing the system to function autonomously over long periods of time,
without relying on the goodwill, management, or intervention of individuals.


## Key Characteristics

Kan is not “emptiness” or “nothingness”.

It exists at a structural layer beyond religion, morality, or ideology.

Compassion is not assumed as a premise;  
it emerges as a result.

The absence of administrators, founders, or virtuous individuals is permitted.

Individual implementations and operating entities may fail, cease operation, or deviate from their intended course.

However, the principles of CP are not lost and may be inherited by future generations.


## Kan within CP

Within CP, Kan is the completed state in which
prayer (sorrow) and strategy are embedded into the institutional structure,
without presupposing the continued presence of the initiator,
administrators, or virtuous individuals of that locality,
while continuing to function as compassion.

## 環 – kan –

CPは哀しみから生まれ  
祈りを構造に沈め環-kan-として閉じられる  
もう誰も同じ哀しみを背負わなくていいようにと


環とは、  
完成された循環構造において、隠された戦略が構造そのものに沈み込み、  
個人の善意・管理・介入を前提とせず、長期にわたり自律的に機能し続ける状態を指す。


## 要点明示

環は「空」や「無」ではない  
宗教・道徳・思想を超えた構造階層にある  
慈愛は前提ではなく結果として現れる  
管理者・創始者・善人の不在を許容する  
個別の実装や運営体は破綻・停止・逸脱し得る  
しかしCPの原理は失われず、後の時代に継承され得る


## CPにおける環

CPにおける環とは、  
祈り（哀しみ）と戦略が制度構造に沈み、  
その土地の起動者・管理者・善人の継続的存在を前提とせず 
慈愛として振る舞い続ける完成状態である。
